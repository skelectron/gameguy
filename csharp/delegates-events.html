Events: safe subscriptions

Events decouple systems. The Player doesn't know that the HUD, the audio system and the achievement tracker are all listening.

Reading BRUTAL's OnKey

Internally, BRUTAL receives the native GLFW callback, converts the raw ints into C# enums, and raises the event. If your handler throws, the exception is collected (by Glfw.ExceptionAggregator) instead of crashing inside native code. Throwing an exception through native C frames is undefined behaviour.

Performance notes

  • Creating a lambda that captures variables allocates a closure object. Do it once at setup, not every frame.
  • Lambdas that capture nothing are cached by the compiler. Mark them static (static t => t * t) to have the compiler enforce that.
  • Always -= handlers from long-lived publishers. A forgotten subscription keeps the subscriber alive, which is a classic leak.
