GameMaker

LicenseProprietary. Free for non-commercial use, with paid licences for commercial and console releases. Check current terms on gamemaker.io.
LanguageGML (GameMaker Language), a C-like scripting language, or visual GML
StrengthsExtremely fast 2D workflow, built-in sprite editor, a long track record of hit indie games
PlatformsDesktop, mobile, Web and consoles

Mental model: objects, events, rooms

  • Sprites: images and animations, made in the built-in editor or imported.
  • Objects: blueprints with events (Create, Step, Draw, Collision with X, Key Pressed…).
  • Rooms: levels where you place object instances and tile layers.

A Step event

Look familiar? It's the axis-separated collision from Coin Dash. GameMaker games traditionally run at a fixed room speed (e.g. 60 steps per second), so values are "per step" rather than "per second".
