Sprite animation

Frames, poses and timing

  • Idle: 2–4 frames at 4–6 FPS. Gentle breathing or bobbing.
  • Run: 4–8 frames at 10–12 FPS. Contact, down, passing and up poses.
  • Jump: separate single frames for rising, peak and falling, chosen from vertical velocity rather than time.
  • Attack: anticipation (a slow wind-up) → action (1 fast frame) → follow-through (recovery).

Frames don't all have to last the same time. Holding a key pose longer adds weight and punch.
