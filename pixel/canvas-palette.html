SizeFeels likeGood for

32×32Detailed 16-bit and modern indieCharacters with expression, detailed tiles
64×64+Hi-bit, illustration-likePortraits, bosses, fighting games

Bigger isn't better. Every doubling means four times as many pixels to draw for every animation frame.

Pick a game resolution

Choose a virtual resolution that scales to common screens by whole numbers:

  • 320×180: ×4 = 720p, ×6 = 1080p, ×8 = 1440p, ×12 = 4K. (Coin Dash uses 320×176 plus a letterbox.)
  • 640×360: ×2 = 720p, ×3 = 1080p, ×4 = 1440p.
  • 256×224 / 256×240: classic 4:3 console sizes.

In BRUTAL with SDL, SetRenderLogicalPresentation(renderer, 320, 180, IntegerScale) handles this for you (see Pixel-perfect 2D with SDL).

Building colour ramps

A ramp is a set of shades of one material, running from dark to light. Good ramps don't just change brightness. They shift hue: shadows lean toward cool colours (blue or purple) and highlights toward warm ones (yellow). This "hue shifting" makes art feel alive instead of muddy.
