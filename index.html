GameGuy teaches game development from the ground up. The main track is BRUTAL, the open-source C# framework that RimWorld's Waylith engine and Kitten Space Agency are built on. Around it are lessons on modern C#, how engines are built, game design and pixel art.

Why BRUTAL?

Most engines give you an editor, a scene graph and a renderer, and you build inside them. BRUTAL gives you fast, allocation-aware C# building blocks instead: windowing, Vulkan/OpenGL, ImGui, Box2D, Jolt, audio and networking. You put them together into the game, or the engine, you want.

That makes it a great way to learn how games really work, with no "magic" hidden behind an editor.
