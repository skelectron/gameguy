FLOAT GOTCHAS

0.1f + 0.2f is not exactly 0.3f. Never compare floats with == for gameplay. Use a tolerance (MathF.Abs(a - b) < 0.0001f). Also, 5 / 2 is 2 (integer division). Write 5 / 2f to get 2.5.
