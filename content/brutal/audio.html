Sound with OpenAL

You will learn
  • The OpenAL model: device → context → buffers and sources
  • How to generate a sound effect from raw PCM samples
  • Your three audio options in BRUTAL: OpenAL, SDL Mixer and FMOD

The OpenAL model

  • Device: an audio output, such as your speakers.
  • Context: the "world" of sound on that device, which also holds the listener position.
  • Buffer: raw audio data, loaded once.
  • Source: something that is playing a buffer, at a position and with a volume and pitch. You can play the same buffer from many sources at once.
