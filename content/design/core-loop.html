Core loops & player goals

A core loop is the set of actions the player repeats most often. If the loop isn't fun, nothing built on top of it will save the game.

Three nested loops

Long-termhours to weeksunlock worlds, master every levelgrow the colony, build a ship and escape

Goal → challenge → reward

  1. Goal: the player knows what they want (that coin up on the ledge).
  2. Challenge: getting there takes skill or a decision (a tricky jump).
  3. Reward: success feels good (a sound, a sparkle, the score going up) and points toward the next goal.

Rewards that open up new goals keep the loop turning: a new ability, a new area, or a new problem to solve.

Write your loop in one sentence

TEMPLATE

"The player [verb] to [goal] while avoiding [obstacle], earning [reward] that lets them [new verb/goal]."

Coin Dash: "The player runs and jumps to collect every coin while avoiding pits, earning a faster time that lets them chase a medal."

Verbs first

List your game's verbs (jump, dash, build, trade, sneak). Fewer, deeper verbs usually beat many shallow ones. A great platformer can be built on just run and jump, with levels that keep finding new uses for them.
