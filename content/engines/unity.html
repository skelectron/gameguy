Mental model: GameObjects and components

A scene contains GameObjects. Each one gets its behaviour from attached components: Transform, SpriteRenderer, Rigidbody2D and your own MonoBehaviour scripts. This is the component-composition model from Entities. Unity's DOTS adds a true ECS for high-performance work.
