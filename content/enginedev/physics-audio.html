Integrating physics & audio

You'll almost never write a rigid-body solver or an audio mixer yourself. Engine work here is about integration: connecting a library such as Brutal.Box2D, Brutal.Jolt or Brutal.OpenAl to your entities, loop and assets.

Physics integration checklist

  1. Step in the fixed update. world.Step(STEP, subSteps) goes inside the accumulator loop.
  2. One owner of truth. For physics-driven entities, the physics body owns the position. Copy body.GetTransform() into the render transform after stepping, and never write positions in both places.
  3. Units. Physics works in metres (0.1–10 m objects) and rendering in pixels. Convert in exactly one place.
  4. Kinematic vs dynamic. Player characters are often kinematic (moved by your code) or custom AABB controllers, while crates and debris are dynamic.
  5. Contacts → gameplay events. Read the begin-touch and sensor events after each step and turn them into game events (CoinCollected, PlayerHurt).
  6. Lifetime. Destroying an entity destroys its body. Disposing the world (e.g. on scene exit) destroys all of them.

Custom collision vs a physics engine

Use hand-written AABB/tile collisionUse Box2D/Jolt
Platformers, top-down action, grid gamesStacking, ragdolls, vehicles, ropes, destruction
You want exact, "unrealistic" controlYou want emergent, realistic motion
Simple and fully deterministicLots of features; tuning matters

Many games use both: a custom controller for the player and Box2D for the props.

Audio integration

  • Buffers are assets. Load and decode each one once through the resource manager, and refer to it with a handle.
  • Sources are voices. Keep a pool of, say, 32 OpenAL sources. To play a sound, take a free voice; if none is free, steal the quietest or oldest one.
  • Buses. Group sounds into Master, Music, SFX and UI, each with a volume. The final volume is sfx × master × userSetting.
  • Positional audio. Set each source's position and the listener's position (usually the camera) every frame.
  • Music streaming. Queue several small buffers and refill them as they finish, instead of loading 5 minutes of PCM at once.
  • Gameplay triggers, audio reacts. Gameplay raises CoinCollected and the audio system plays the sound. Gameplay code never calls OpenAL.

When shutting down, delete the sources with Al.DeleteSources(_sources) before the context is destroyed, as in HelloOpenAl.cs.
